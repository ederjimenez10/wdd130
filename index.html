Venezuela

Venezuela is a South American country known for its diverse landscapes, including the Andes Mountains, the Amazon rainforest, and Caribbean beaches. It has the world's largest proven oil reserves, which have significantly shaped its economy and political history. The country has a rich cultural heritage, with influences from indigenous, Spanish, and African traditions.
